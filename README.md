# 📚 Bahasa Melayu Leksikon — BMCS2013 Data Engineering Assignment

A data engineering project focused on developing a **Bahasa Melayu Leksikon** using Python. The project aims to collect, process, and organize Malay vocabulary together with its meanings and related linguistic information.

By combining **web crawling, web scraping, and API integration**, we gather vocabulary data and enrich it with additional information to build a structured Malay lexicon.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Collect Bahasa Melayu vocabulary from online sources.
- Extract word-related information through web crawling and web scraping.
- Integrate APIs to retrieve word meanings and additional linguistic information.
- Organize and structure the collected data into a searchable lexicon.
- Improve the completeness and quality of the lexicon through continuous updates.

## 👨‍💻 My Individual Contributions

My individual responsibilities focused on evaluating the lexicon's coverage, analyzing synonym relationships, and improving the quality of the collected data.

### 1. 📊 Coverage Analysis

Examined the coverage of articles within our lexicon to evaluate how well the collected vocabulary represents the content of a given article.

**Main focus:**
- Analyze vocabulary coverage in an article.
- Identify words that are already available in the lexicon.
- Examine words that are missing from the lexicon.
- Evaluate the completeness of the lexicon based on article content.

### 2. 🕸️ Network of Synonyms

Examined the relationships between synonyms in the lexicon by analyzing how words are connected through their similar meanings.

**Main focus:**
- Identify synonym relationships between words.
- Analyze connections between vocabulary items.
- Examine the synonym network and its structure.
- Understand how words are semantically related.
